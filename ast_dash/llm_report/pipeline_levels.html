Home Dynamics Pipeline

Five levels turn raw AudioSet rows into statements about people. Here is one mock evening pushed through all of them, with the real output of each stage.

RAW→ L0 observations→ L1 semantic events→ L1.5 semantic interaction→ L2 activity episodes→ L3 person & social→ L4 daily dynamics

How the levels connect

The shape that matters is not the list but the branch. L1.5 does not sit in the main line — it takes its turns from L0 and rejoins at L3, alongside the activity path rather than inside it. And one arrow carries the whole system's weakness: everything above L2 is joined on a speaker's name.

L0
Canonical observations
11 rows in, 11 out

Normalise the export. Nothing is interpreted here — every field is either copied, parsed, or recorded as missing.

  1. 1Timestamps parsed from any of three formats, emitted as ISO. Unparseable rows are kept but sorted last.
  2. 2Labels read from slots 1–3 with their probabilities. A nullish value — "", none, nan, unknown — is dropped into raw_labels_discarded rather than deleted, so the discard is auditable.
  3. 3Room resolved in order: the event's own field, then device_room_map, then unknown. Which one won is recorded in room_source.
  4. 4Diarisation is parsed out of the transcription string, because the pipeline writes speaker identity into the text rather than a column. Markers are matched anywhere, and each turn's words run to the next marker.
  5. 5Two speech flags, deliberately separate: has_speech means a transcription exists at all; has_words means the recogniser produced words. A clip can be the first without the second.
  6. 6Emotion parsed from "HAP (0.71)" into a label and a confidence.

L1
Semantic events
11 observations → 40 events

Translate each observation into meaning, through three independent extractors. Nothing above this level knows which modality produced what — this is where a future camera or door sensor would join.

  1. AAudio labels. Each AudioSet label maps to zero or more semantic types through a table that is data, not logic. Several labels hitting one type combine: the strongest wins, plus a small corroboration bonus, capped at 0.95. An unmapped label becomes unknown_activity and carries its own uncertainty note. A label mapped to the empty list is deliberately ignored.
  2. BDiarisation. Independent of the labels, because the AudioSet head often scores Speech low on recordings that plainly contain words. Emits speech_activity at 0.90 with words or 0.70 without, one identified_speaker per named speaker, and conversation_candidate when two or more named speakers share a recording.
  3. CEmotion. Passed up as emotion_evidence carrying the model's own confidence — evidence, never a conclusion.

L1.5
Semantic interaction
576 exchanges in 5 days

Read what a spoken exchange meant between people. This is the one interpretive stage — it needs a language model, so the model is passed in and the rest of the pipeline never depends on it.

L1 cannot make this distinction, and neither can anything above it. "I love you" and "you idiot" are both speech_activity plus conversation_candidate plus two identified_speakers. They arrive at L3 as the same record, and the difference is gone before L4 ever sees it.

  1. 1Cluster turns into exchanges — same room, within 120 s — using the same function L3 uses, so both levels agree on what an exchange is.
  2. 2Gate on intelligibility. Eight words for one stretch of speech, or four across a genuine two-voice back-and-forth, because a reply is evidence in itself. Below that nothing is sent and nothing is spent.
  3. 3Interpret the exchange, with neighbouring turns as read-only context. One call per exchange, cached by content hash.
  4. 4Validate the reply. Speakers come from diarisation only — an invented name cannot enter. Unknown interaction types collapse to other. Scores offered alongside an abstention are discarded.
  5. 5Abstain honestly. null means no evidence; 0.0 means evidence of absence. On uninterpretable speech every dimension is null, never zero.
Why repair is kept separately

An apology after an argument does not cancel the argument. The episode keeps the tension peak, the repair that followed, and the ordered sequence between them — so "conflict load" and "repair rate" stay separately measurable instead of averaging into one lukewarm number.

L2
Activity episodes
40 events → 12 episodes

Merge semantic events that are close in time, room and meaning, so that "breathing, then water, then a flush" becomes one thing that happened rather than three readings.

  1. 1Group by (room, semantic type) and split whenever the gap exceeds that type's allowance — 120 s for speech, 300 s for water, 600 s for cooking and media.
  2. 2Supporting types never define an episode. identified_speaker, emotion_evidence, human_presence_candidate, movement_candidate and friends describe how something was observed, so they fold into whichever episode they overlap but never start one.
  3. 3Composite rules relabel and nudge. When the types present satisfy a rule — and, for some rules, the room matches — the episode is renamed and its confidence boosted. First match wins.

L3
Person & social episodes
→ 15 episodes

The boundary of the whole system. Below here everything is keyed on sound; from here up, everything is keyed on a person. Six episode types are produced, all of them joined on the speaker name.

  1. 1person_presence_evidence — one per observation per named speaker. Confidence 0.80 for a configured household member, 0.50 for a stranger. Always carries "presence at this moment only; continuous presence not implied".
  2. 2speech_episode — turns clustered while consecutive turns are within 120 s and in the same room. Records turn count, per-speaker turn counts, speaker changes, transcript excerpts and an emotion summary.
  3. 3reciprocal_conversation_candidate — fires only when two or more known speakers are in one cluster. Adds turn balance, response gaps, and a confidence that rises 0.05 per speaker change.
  4. 4single_person_speech_episode — exactly one known speaker, tagged "does not imply the person was alone".
  5. 5same_room_copresence_candidate — two different members with presence evidence in the same room within 300 s, merged into windows so a run of alternating speech yields one episode, not one per pair of rows.
  6. 6shared_media_candidate — an L2 media episode with two or more people nearby. With one person it becomes media_use_with_unidentified_viewers; with none, plain media_use.

Event 108, concluded

Its turn did join the living-room speech cluster — turn_count: 3 counts it, and its '...' appears in the excerpts. But it contributed no presence episode, no participant, and nothing to any pair, because it had no name to join on. It inflates the appearance of activity while adding nothing a person-level conclusion can be built from.

That is the exact mechanism by which the measured 42% Unknown rate becomes missing relationships rather than visible gaps. And because reciprocal_conversation_candidate requires two known speakers, a real exchange with one end unattributed does not degrade — it vanishes.

L4
Daily dynamics
→ 2 day records

Aggregate one day at a time, per person and per pair, and state the limits of the evidence alongside it. This is the object the LLM actually reads.

  1. 1household_activity — episode counts by activity and by room.
  2. 2individual_patterns — per member: speech episodes, presence evidence, rooms recognised in, an emotion distribution, and a breakdown of social participation. Only people the day actually evidences appear.
  3. 3pair_dynamics — per pair: conversations, co-presence, shared activities, a mean turn balance, a joint emotion summary, and an evidence_strength of "moderate" at three or more shared episodes, otherwise "limited".
  4. 4no_observation_intervals — every gap over 30 minutes, carried up with the standing note that this is absence of evidence, not evidence of silence.
  5. 5data_quality and uncertainties — how many events had no room, how much speech went unattributed, whether coverage is known. Shipped as part of the payload, not as a footnote.
  6. 6baseline_comparison — against prior days, and refuses to compare below seven days of history.

What the design gets right

Event 108 is not swept away. It appears at L4 as unidentified_speech_event_count: 1, and the four observation gaps are reported as gaps rather than as a quiet house. The pipeline tells the model what it could not see — which is precisely what stops missing evidence being read as evidence of absence.

Reading it against the evaluation

Levels 0 through 2 are keyed on sound, and the components feeding them measured well: sound labelling is correct at rank one on 82.4% of clips, and the speech gate drops no speech at all. Levels 3 and 4 are keyed on a person, and the component feeding them — speaker identity — measured 51.1%.

Event 108 is that gap in miniature. The pipeline handles it correctly at every step and still cannot produce a person from it, because there is no name to join on. Everything the daily report says about individuals and pairs is built on a join key that is currently right about half the time, and the failure is silent: a conversation with one end unattributed does not arrive marked uncertain, it does not arrive at all.

The one design decision that changes this is the speaker abstain. With it, unattributed speech becomes unidentified_speech_event_count — visible, countable, and correctly excluded from pair dynamics. Without it, every clip gets a name, and those same clips arrive at L4 as participants in conversations that may not have happened.
